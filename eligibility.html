Illustrative estimate only, based on general 2026 Malaysian market practice: banks commonly discount variable income (commission ~20%, own business ~30%, gig/platform work ~30–40% without a filed tax return) when assessing DSR, and DSR comfort levels are typically under 60% (rising to around 65% under the government's SJKP / SJKP MADANI guarantee scheme for first-time buyers of homes priced up to RM500,000, including gig and self-employed applicants). Every bank sets its own exact rules and documentation requirements — this tool is a guide to start the conversation, not an approval. Confirm your real numbers with Felix and your bank.

DSR = total monthly commitments (incl. new instalment) ÷ income counted for DSR purposes. NDI = income counted − total monthly commitments.
