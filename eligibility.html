Illustrative estimate only, based on general 2026 Malaysian bank practice: variable income is usually discounted (commission ~20%, own business ~30%, gig/platform work ~30–40% without a filed tax return), bonus/allowance is usually counted at ~50%, and approvals typically need DSR under 60% (around 65% under SJKP / SJKP MADANI for first-time buyers of homes up to RM500,000). Every bank has its own rules — this is a starting point, not a loan approval. Confirm with Felix and your bank.

DSR = all monthly commitments (incl. new instalment) ÷ income counted by the bank.
